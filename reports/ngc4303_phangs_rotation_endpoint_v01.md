# NGC4303 PHANGS rotation endpoint v01

Status: `NUMERIC_ENDPOINT_ACQUIRED_QUALITY_LIMITED_SCORING_CLOSED`

The checksum-frozen official ECSV contains `43` NGC4303 points over `0.125-6.425 kpc`. Lang et al. do not mark NGC4303 as an unreliable orientation fit, but give `i=23.5+/-9.2 deg` and truncate the velocity field at `60 arcsec` because incomplete outer coverage made bins minor-axis sensitive.

The endpoint is now open. No new operator may be selected from its values. PHANGS and COMING both use CO, so this is not a strict independent-tracer validation endpoint.
