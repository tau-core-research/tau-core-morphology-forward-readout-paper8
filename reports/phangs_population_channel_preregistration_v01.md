# PHANGS population channel preregistration v01

Status: `SOURCE_FROZEN_POPULATION_TEST_PREREGISTERED_ENDPOINTS_CLOSED`

The source-only rule retains `IC5332, NGC4254, NGC4321`. `NGC4254` is a legacy pilot; the unopened confirmatory cohort is `IC5332, NGC4321`.

For every eligible nonbarred body, `m0+m1` is nuisance and `m2` is the sole test family. Promotion requires global confirmatory `p<0.01` and individual `m2 p<0.05` in both confirmatory galaxies. Failure preserves the null and does not authorize another post-result galaxy choice.
