# UGC08490 tracer-scale origin audit v01

Status: `NEGATIVE_RESULT_PRESERVED`

The measured H I/Halpha scale is `0.8154` with a point-bootstrap 95% interval of `[0.7657, 0.8631]`. GHASP used a kinematic inclination of `40 +/- 15 deg`, while Repetto et al. used `50.3 +/- 2.0 deg`. Pure deprojection therefore predicts `sin(40)/sin(50.3) = 0.8354`.

Using the inclination-only factor lowers RMS from `14.64` to `6.55 km/s`; a freely fitted scale reaches `6.39 km/s`. The remaining multiplicative factor after inclination harmonization is `0.9760`.

The scale is a real difference between the published readouts, but this audit does not promote it as a Tau Core channel signal: the independently documented inclination mismatch already predicts its magnitude.
