# NGC7479 H I radial proxy v01

Status: `PUBLICATION_RASTER_GAS_PROXY_RECONSTRUCTED_NOT_FITS_GRADE`

The embedded 174x207 grayscale moment-0 image is isolated from the PDF, registered with the published coordinate bounds and kinematic centre, deprojected at `i=51 deg`, `PA=22 deg`, and normalized to the published H I mass. Three grayscale-floor sensitivities are preserved.

The proxy conserves total H I+helium mass but publication grayscale linearity is not proven. It is suitable for pipeline diagnostics only; precision endpoint scoring remains blocked until the calibrated digital moment-0/FITS product is recovered or validates this reconstruction.
