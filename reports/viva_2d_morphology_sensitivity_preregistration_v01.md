Status: `SOURCE_FROZEN_CONVENTIONAL_SENSITIVITY_CONTROL_READY_FOR_ACQUISITION`

Before opening or downloading any of the six velocity cubes, this packet freezes an exact three-disturbed/three-quiet VIVA cohort from the morphology and interaction descriptions in Chung et al. (2009). The source-side m=1 H I morphology is tested only through its k=2 line-of-sight upper sideband after per-annulus systemic, circular, and radial nuisance removal.

The primary statistic is matched-template specificity relative to radial-reversal, annular-phase, and cross-galaxy controls. Exact 3-of-6 label permutation is primary; with only 20 assignments, p=0.05 is the strongest attainable result. Failure demotes this terminal, not Tau Core. Success would demonstrate conventional morphology-correlated kinematic sensitivity only.

The cohort and all reduction, rank, missing-data, and inference rules are machine-frozen in `data/derived/viva_2d_morphology_sensitivity_preregistration_v01.json` and `data/derived/viva_2d_morphology_sensitivity_preregistration_v01.csv`.
