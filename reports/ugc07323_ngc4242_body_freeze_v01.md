# UGC07323 / NGC4242 body freeze v01

Status: `SOURCE_NATIVE_QUALITATIVE_RING_PLUS_APPROACHING_EXTENSION_BODY_FROZEN`

Richards et al. Appendix A19 and the public WHISP overview independently support
an inner H I ring and a southwest H I extension localized to the approaching
side; the extension is also visible in narrow-band Halpha. The H I field is
solid-body-like, while ionized-gas kinematics are similar but more irregular.

No reliable numeric onset is available from the current sources. Therefore no
radial window, signed correction, or body-conditioned side function is allowed.
The next test is restricted to the already derived zero-parameter reciprocal-
load compatibility over full common support.
