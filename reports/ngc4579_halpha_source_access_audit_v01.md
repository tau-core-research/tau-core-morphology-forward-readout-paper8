# NGC4579 Halpha source-access audit v01

## Superseding outcome

The full published PDF was subsequently acquired from HAL and checksum-frozen.
Figure A26 exposes the black source-model line on the approaching post-bar side,
but the line exits through the upper PV-panel boundary before the receding
post-bar domain. The prefrozen requirement of at least three bins on both sides
therefore fails. No score is permitted from this graphical endpoint; a numeric
rotation product or a newly prefrozen velocity-field extraction is required.

## Numeric replacement carrier

The Daigle et al. (2006) SINGS paper identifies an author electronic rotation-
curve catalogue. Its archived index and `ngc4579.dat` file were recovered from
the Internet Archive. The checksum-frozen table contains 35 numeric Halpha
rotation points with `side_error`, `gipsy_error`, and source-selected
`final_error`; 20 points lie beyond the fixed `3.62 kpc` bar radius. Velocities
are source-averaged across sides, so this route is retained as a caveated
prospective operator replay rather than a full side-resolved endpoint.

Status: `SOURCE_CONFIRMED_NUMERIC_OR_FULL_FIGURE_PRODUCT_ACCESS_BLOCKED`

Chemin et al. include NGC4579, but not NGC4303, in the Virgo Fabry-Perot Halpha
atlas. For NGC4579 they report `PA_kin=91+/-3 deg`, `i_kin=44+/-8 deg`, good
seeing, and a central nuclear-spiral twist. Their geometry is taken from the
radial range with the most stable angles.

The accessible arXiv package deliberately includes only the NGC4254 example.
The published legacy FTP full-figure route currently rejects anonymous access,
and no numeric rotation table was found. No NGC4579 Halpha rotation value has
been opened, so the prospective freeze remains intact. Scoring is closed.
