# EDGE--CALIFA rotation/morphology preregistration v01

Status: `SOURCE_ONLY_PROTOCOL_FROZEN_FULL_VELOCITY_ENDPOINT_UNOPENED`

The full public matched-resolution velocity endpoint has not been acquired or opened. The protocol excludes the 17 published Levy et al. kinematic-subsample galaxies, freezes source-only eligibility and a deterministic development/confirmation split, and constructs a five-zone `m1+m2` external operational homolog.  It is not an exact replication because CALIFA/Pipe3D stellar maps and CARMA CO(1--0) replace the S4G and PHANGS CO(2--1) source products.

The primary question is deliberately conditional: after projecting out a declared eDIG/ionized-gas source span, does the correct stellar-plus-CO morphology explain the CO-minus-Halpha velocity field better than equal-rank radial-reversal, phase-rotation, and cross-galaxy controls?

A pass would be a source-frozen prevalidation signal, not a unique Tau Core or dark-sector detection, because no universal body-to-terminal coefficient law has yet been derived.
