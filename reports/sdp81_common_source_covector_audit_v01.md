# SDP.81 common source-covector audit v01

The minimal relation `grad_theta I_i = A_i^T grad_beta I_source` was fitted jointly to all four q1 paths over two registration anchors and three beam-scaled apertures. The relative residual spans `0.328` to `0.614`.

One common local scalar covector is therefore not robustly promoted across the complete sensitivity set. This does not reject the beam-convolved extended-source reconstruction or a richer Tau body covector.
