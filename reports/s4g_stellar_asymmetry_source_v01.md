**Status:** `SOURCE_ONLY_S4G_STELLAR_ASYMMETRY_ACQUIRED`

VizieR `J/ApJ/781/12/galaxies`. The selected coordinate is the rotational asymmetry
`A`, measured independently in the 3.6 and 4.5 micron images.
