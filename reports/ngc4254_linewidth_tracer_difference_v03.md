# NGC4254 line-width versus tracer-difference audit v03

The official PHANGS CO strict moment-2 and equivalent-width maps are now checksum-frozen. Across five nonreference annuli, absolute CO-Halpha common-mode difference versus CO moment-2 gives Spearman `rho=-0.600` (`p=0.285`); versus observed Halpha sigma it gives `rho=0.000` (`p=1.000`).
